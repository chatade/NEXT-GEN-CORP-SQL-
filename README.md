# NextGen HR Analytics - PostgreSQL

## Overview

This project showcases PostgreSQL skill by analyzing HR data for **NextGen Corp.**, a fictional technology company facing challenges related to employee retention, performance management, and salary fairness.

Using PostgreSQL, I restored the provided database, wrote analytical SQL queries, and generated business insights that support HR decision-making. The project demonstrates my ability to work with relational databases, perform data analysis, and answer real-world business questions using SQL.


## Business Problem

As NextGen Corp. continues to grow, the HR team requires data-driven insights to improve workforce management. The company aims to:

* Improve employee retention by identifying turnover trends.
* Monitor employee performance across departments.
* Evaluate salary fairness based on performance and job roles.
* Support strategic HR decisions through data analysis.

## Database Structure

The project uses the following tables:

Table           Description                                                                           
 **Employees**    Employee details including job title, department, salary, hire date, attendance rate,                     and performance score. 
 
**Departments**   List of company departments.                                                                                 
**Performance**   Monthly employee performance records.                                                                        
 **Attendance**   Employee attendance history.                                                                               
 **Turnover**     Employees who left the company and their reasons for leaving.                                                
**Salaries**     Historical salary records and salary changes.                                                               

## Project Objectives

### Employee Retention Analysis

The objective of this analysis is to understand employee turnover patterns and identify factors contributing to employee attrition.

Key business questions include:

* Identifying the top 5 longest-serving employees.
* Calculating turnover rates for each department.
* Identifying employees who may be at risk of leaving based on performance.
* Determining the most common reasons employees leave the company.

### Performance Analysis

This section evaluates employee performance across departments to identify high-performing teams and areas requiring improvement.

Business questions include:

* Total number of employees who have left the company.
* Number of employees with a performance score of **5.0**.
* Number of employees with performance scores **below 3.5**.
* Departments with the highest concentration of top and low performers.
* Average performance score by department.

### Salary Analysis

The salary analysis focuses on compensation trends and salary fairness within the organization.

Business questions include:

* Total company salary expenditure.
* Average salary by job title.
* Number of employees earning above **$80,000**.
* Relationship between employee performance and salary across departments.
